## 📌 Project Overview

The **PR.2 Sales Analyzer** uses Excel's data-analysis features to turn sales records into meaningful business insights. It includes a summary dashboard, customer analysis, What-If scenarios, regression analysis, descriptive statistics, monthly growth tracking, and PivotTable summaries.

The workbook is designed to help users understand **what is selling, where sales are strongest, which customers are most valuable, and how discounts may affect profit**.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- 📈 Analyze total sales and profit performance.
- 🏆 Identify high-value customers and top purchasers.
- 🌎 Compare sales across regions and product categories.
- 🧮 Explore the potential impact of discount changes with What-If Analysis.
- 📉 Examine the relationship between sales and profit using linear regression.
- 📊 Summarize key findings with charts and a dashboard.
- ✅ Validate calculations with reconciliation and error checks.

## 🔍 Analysis Included

### 1. 🏆 Customer Analysis
- Highlights top customers by total purchase.
- Uses customer-level summaries to identify high-value customers.
- Helps compare customer contribution to total sales.

### 2. 🎟️ What-If Analysis
- Tests how changing the discount assumption may affect estimated profit.
- Makes scenario assumptions visible so results can be interpreted carefully.

### 3. 📉 Linear Regression
- Analyzes the relationship between **Sales** and **Profit**.
- Uses Excel's Data Analysis ToolPak regression output.

### 4. 📐 Descriptive Statistics
- Summarizes key statistical properties of the numeric data.
- Helps understand the distribution and variation in the dataset.

### 5. 📅 Monthly Growth
- Tracks monthly sales.
- Uses arrows or symbols to make increases and decreases easier to spot.

### 6. 📊 Pivot Summary & Charts
- Compares sales by region and product category.
- Uses bar, line, and pie charts to communicate trends and proportions.

### 7. ✅ Data Integrity Checks
- Checks whether summary totals reconcile with the dataset.
- Includes checks for blank cells and error values.

## 🚀 How to Use

1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Open the Excel workbook (`2. Analyzer.xlsx`) in Microsoft Excel.
3. Start with the **Dashboard** sheet for a quick overview.
4. Review the `Dataset` sheet to understand the source records.
5. Explore the customer, What-If, regression, statistics, monthly growth, and Pivot summary sheets.
6. If you update the source data, review the formulas and refresh relevant PivotTables/charts.
7. Save a copy before making major changes.

> **Note:** The `NOW()` formula is volatile and may update when Excel recalculates the workbook. Some features, including the Analysis ToolPak and PivotTables, may require desktop Excel and may need to be refreshed after changing data.
